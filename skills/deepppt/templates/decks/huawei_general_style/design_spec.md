# Huawei General Style Template

> Standard replication from hw_template.pptx (149 slides, 5 masters, 12 layouts). Extracted representative page types covering all distinct layout structures.

## I. Template Overview

A clean, professional enterprise presentation template styled in Huawei's signature red-white corporate palette. Primary accent #E9002F (Huawei Red) against white backgrounds. Designed for strategic reporting, product launches, solution introductions, and executive briefings at Huawei and partner organizations.

**Target audience**: Executives, product managers, solution architects, enterprise clients

**Design voice**: Professional, authoritative, structured, data-driven

## III. Signature Design Elements

- **Red brand bar**: Full-width #E9002F accent line or header band
- **White background**: Clean white with subtle card shadows/borders (#DDDDDD)
- **Split layouts**: Left-right, top-bottom grid structures
- **Typography**: Arial (Latin) / 微软雅黑 (Chinese), hierarchy via size and weight
- **Minimal decoration**: No gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects — professional restraint
