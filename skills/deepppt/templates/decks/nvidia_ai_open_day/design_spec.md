# NVIDIA AI Open Day Template

> Standard replication from "NVIDIA AI Open Day - 5 sessions.pdf"

## I. Template Overview

A bold, dark-themed presentation template inspired by NVIDIA's signature design language. Bright green (#76B900) against deep black (#000000) creates high-contrast visual impact suitable for AI/GPU product launches, technical keynotes, and developer conferences.
**Target audience**: Developer community, CTO/CIO, AI researchers
**Design voice**: Bold, technical, future-forward, performance-driven

## II. Color Scheme

| Role | HEX | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | #76B900 | NVIDIA Green — titles, highlights, accent bars |
| Background | #000000 | Deep black — cover, section dividers |
| Surface | #111111 | Dark gray — content cards |
| Text Primary | #FFFFFF | White — body text on dark bg |
| Text Secondary | #AAAAAA | Light gray — subtitles, captions |
| Accent | #1A1A2E | Dark navy — secondary surface |

## III. Signature Design Elements

- Full-black backgrounds with bright green accent bars
- Geometric line patterns (GPU architecture motifs)
- Large bold typography for impact statements
- Clean card-based content layout
- Green gradient bar at page tops
